RULES

Objective

Use your 10 pieces to control possession, create passing opportunities, block the opponent and score more goals before full-time. Secondary Points reward possession wins and qualifying passes.

+3Goal
+1Take Possession
+1Pass when passer & receiver are both in opposition half

Square = Defence, Circle = Midfield, Triangle = Attack.

Movement

  • + moves horizontally or vertically, any legal distance.
  • ♦ moves horizontally, vertically or diagonally, any legal distance.
  • Defence stays in its own half.
  • Midfield can operate in both halves.
  • Attack can enter the attacking half; once it does, it cannot return.
  • Attack may skip Attack and Midfield pieces from either team.
  • Defence pieces block an Attack piece's movement path.
  • A piece cannot finish a move on an occupied square.

Possession

  • The red ring shows which piece has possession.
  • A pass changes possession only; the pieces do not move.
  • Passes can travel horizontally, vertically or diagonally.
  • Opponents block a passing lane; your own teammates do not.
  • Take Possession earns +1.
  • A piece must already be in a legal taking position when the turn begins. It cannot move into position and then Take Possession in the same turn.
  • A + piece may Take Possession from farther away when aligned in the same row or column and both pieces are in the same half.

Scoring

  • Only a possession piece may enter the opponent's gold scoring block.
  • No piece may enter its own scoring block.
  • A goal earns +3.
  • If the possession piece already moved with possession during that turn, it cannot later move into the scoring block during the same turn.

Match Structure

  • 5-minute match: two halves of 2:30.
  • Each turn gives 3 actions and up to 30 seconds.
  • At half-time the board restarts from the chosen formation.
  • The opponent/Orange side starts the second half with possession.
  • Scores continue through half-time.
